# Thesis framing

- The thesis studies Kalshi sports contracts across MLB, NBA and NFL. Preserve this multi-sport framing in introductions, literature synthesis, motivation and conclusions.
- Introduce general questions through game events, scoring plays and information arrival across sports. Avoid framing the thesis as exclusively about football or any other single sport.
- Association-football goal studies and tennis studies are supporting literature from specific sporting settings. Identify those settings explicitly and connect their findings to the broader research question without implying that they define the thesis's empirical scope.
- Distinguish association football in cited studies from American football (NFL) in the thesis data.
