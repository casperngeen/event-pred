5  SIGN vs MAGNITUDE on trigger-adjacent cells
==============================================================================
Stage-1's signal is in the DIRECTION of the surprise, not its size
(research_log.md §2). A magnitude regression dilutes a sign-only signal
toward zero — so the model can be 'right' on sign and still score R2<0.
