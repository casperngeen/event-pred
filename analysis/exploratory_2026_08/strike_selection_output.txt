==============================================================================
EDGE BY MONEYNESS — sign-invariant bucketing
==============================================================================
Bucketing by ENTRY price is circular: the signal picks the side, the side
sets the entry price, and high-priced contracts mechanically win more.
Bucket by |p0-50| instead, which does not move when the sign flips, and
read the edge as (theory-signed - sign-flipped)/2 within each bucket.

|p0-50|          n   signed  flipped    edge     se    fee  net taker
0-10 (ATM)       7    21.86   -21.86   21.86  18.15   1.71      17.79
10-25           49    12.39   -12.39   12.39   6.30   1.52       8.52
25-40           59     1.32    -1.32    1.32   2.32   0.97      -2.00
40+ (tails)   1154    -0.19     0.19   -0.19   0.14   0.14      -2.68

signed and flipped are exact mirrors, so "edge" is just the signed mean —
the point is that it is NOT the win rate, which the entry-price cut inflates.
