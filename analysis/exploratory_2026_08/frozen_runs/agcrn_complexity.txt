T6  THE SCREENING RULE behind the report's 22 relationships
==============================================================================
Report S4.3.1 calls a pair significant when the 95% CI on the mean
[-3,+14] price change excludes zero for >=3 CONSECUTIVE days. The paths
are cumulative sums normalised to zero at day 0, so consecutive days are
near-perfectly autocorrelated: the consecutive-day rule is not a
multiple-comparison guard. Monte Carlo under a pure random walk:

  pairs n    P(false positive) per pair
        5                         0.183
       10                         0.121
       28                         0.091
      100                         0.078
      811                         0.074

series with >=5 resolved events in the IS panel : 23
ordered trigger->target pairs in the grid       : 506
expected false positives at the measured per-pair rate 0.102 : 52
the report reports                                              : 22

Benjamini-Hochberg on the same grid would need a per-pair p below
   0.05 * 22 / 506 = 0.00217 for the 22nd-ranked pair to survive.
